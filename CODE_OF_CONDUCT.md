# Community expectations

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Critique behavior and code, not people.
Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing and publishing another person's
credentials or private server information are not welcome.

Help newcomers reproduce problems safely. Share synthetic fixtures rather than
production inventories. Give credit to contributors and upstream maintainers.
Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation when necessary.

For moderation concerns, request a private channel through the project maintainers
or [shixpeer.com](https://shixpeer.com). Security issues follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
